## Day 9 — Gan 2026, Fifth Consensus Guidelines (`gan-2026`)

Confirmed by the director from the held full text on 2026-09-06, in response to an external
review that quoted the guideline:

- Guideline 3, verbatim: "ADMINISTER PONV PROPHYLAXIS USING 2 INTERVENTIONS IN ADULTS AT ANY
  RISK."
- Rationale sentence, verbatim: "clinicians' adherence to risk-based adjustments in PONV
  prophylaxis is suboptimal," with the further rationale that modern antiemetics are safe at
  perioperative doses.
- The Apfel score remains validated for estimating risk; it no longer gates whether prophylaxis
  is given.

Not confirmed, and therefore not on any page until round 3 reads the full text: the recommended
doses for dexamethasone and ondansetron; the statement about patients with three or more risk
factors and the paucity of data for three or more interventions; the addition of intravenous
aprepitant and removal of casopitant.
